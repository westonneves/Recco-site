How the answer gets built

An assistant has no directory of businesses.

Every time someone asks, it builds an answer out of whatever it can find about you. Three things decide whether your name is in it.

  1. 01

    It reads

    What's public about you

    Your website, your listings, directories, forums, local news — anything out there that mentions your business. If it is thin, wrong or contradicts itself, the answer is built on that.

  2. 02

    It weighs

    What other people say

    Reviews, and the words inside them, count for more than anything you write about yourself. Recent counts for more than old.

  3. 03

    It answers

    With two or three names

    Each with a reason attached. There is no page two, and nobody can buy a spot in the answer. That is the whole game.
